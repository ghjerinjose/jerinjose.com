Roadmap

Kardashev scale — Type 1 to Type 7.

  1. Type 1 Planetary A civilization that harnesses the full energy of its home planet.
  2. Type 2 Stellar Stellar-scale power — Habitats · Energy · Engines · Star worlds (Dyson-class systems).
  3. Type 3 Galactic A civilization spanning an entire galaxy.
  4. Type 4 Universal Civilization at the scale of the observable universe.
  5. Type 5 Multiversal Operations across many universes.
  6. Type 6 Omniversal Full Omniverse-scale systems and exploration.
  7. Type 7 Beyond omniversal Beyond the Omniverse map — the furthest horizon of expansion and exploration.
